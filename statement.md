# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students often make frequent small purchases across food, transport, study materials, and other campus activities. Without a structured record, it is difficult to know where money is being spent or whether monthly category limits are being exceeded.

## Scope
The project provides a local command-line system for recording expenses, maintaining monthly category budgets, and generating monthly spending reports. It covers CRUD operations, validation, SQLite persistence, budget comparison, and basic analytics. It does not connect to banking services or process real financial transactions.

## Target Users
- College students
- Hostel residents
- Individuals who want a lightweight local expense tracker

## High-Level Features
1. Expense management: add, view, update, and delete expenses.
2. Budget management: set and update monthly category budgets.
3. Reporting and analytics: calculate monthly totals, category totals, highest-spending categories, and budget status.
4. Local persistence: store application data in SQLite.
5. Validation and error handling: reject invalid dates, amounts, months, and menu choices.
